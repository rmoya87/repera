Repera exercise illustrations in this folder are original assets created for Repera.
They are not copies of Gym visual / openGym catalogue media.

Última actualización: 2026-10-17

Canonical image: art_ab_wheel.jpg.
Style: flat anatomical vector; near-black charcoal background; medium-grey
athletic man with no visible facial features and short charcoal hair.
Torso ALWAYS shirtless. Dark charcoal athletic shorts and grey sneakers.
Neon lime is restricted to muscles active in the depicted phase.
No text, logos or gym clutter. Keep camera, anatomy, equipment and body
orientation identical across hero and numbered frames.
Ground oval only when supported. NEVER draw a floor shadow when suspended,
jumping or supported on bar/rings without touching the ground.

Exported assets:
- ExerciseArt/<stem>.jpg: 1024 x 1024 JPEG.
- ExerciseArt/thumbs/<stem>.jpg: 192 x 192 JPEG.
- Export new gens with scripts/export_generated_art.py; avoid homogenizing them.
- Full plus matching thumb are required for every stem.

WARNING: Some existing images are in visual QA quarantine. Presence in this
folder does NOT imply the art is approved. See docs/EXERCISE_ART_AUDIT.csv and
scripts/audit_exercise_art.py. Regenerate, export, inspect and then remove
the corresponding quarantinedSequences entry in ExerciseArt.swift.
