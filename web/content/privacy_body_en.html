Repera (“the App”) is a local-first training app for iPhone and Apple Watch (Android in progress). Site: repera.app. This policy describes what data is processed, for what purposes, and what control you have. By using Repera you accept this policy. If you disagree, do not use the App or the site.

Important: if you connect an AI assistant (e.g. Claude or ChatGPT), part of your training history is mirrored on Repera’s server infrastructure (Supabase) so the assistant can read it or, if you allow writing, propose changes. That flow is not required to train locally.

1. Controller

The controller for processing related to Repera is Ramón de Moya Romero, individual developer and publisher of the App (Spain).

Channel for privacy rights: hola@repera.app (more options on Support).

2. Training without an account (local mode)

You can log workouts on device without creating a Repera account and without connecting an AI assistant. In that mode there is no mandatory multi-user social backend and no Repera server mirror of your history merely because you train.

Optional features (iCloud, Apple Health, Pro purchases, Repera account, AI connection) activate only if you use them.

3. Repera account (Supabase Auth)

Some features—especially the AI assistant connection and Center / business features when you use them—require a Repera account authenticated with Supabase Auth.

Sign-in methods shown in the App and on repera.app/ai:

  • Apple Sign In.
  • Google Sign In.
  • Email and password on repera.app/ai (accounts created by Repera, e.g. the review demo account).

Typical account data: user id, email (if shared by the provider), display name/avatar, session tokens and auth metadata. Apple/Google process sign-in under their own policies; Repera receives the sign-in result.

4. Data stored on your device

Among other items, the App may store locally:

  • Optional profile (display name, photo).
  • Exercises, routines, sessions, sets, loads, RIR/RPE and WOD results.
  • Preferences (units, week start, rest, progression, etc.).
  • Body-weight entries and goals you record.
  • Free-tier usage metadata (e.g. monthly board-scan counter), separate from workouts.
  • Technical caches (e.g. Health data already authorised).
  • If you use Connect your AI: connection state, plan inbox and imports.

This data stays under your control on device. You can export or delete it from App Settings (depending on options in each version).

5. iCloud

If you enable iCloud sync, a copy of your training document may be stored in your iCloud Documents container tied to your Apple ID. Apple processes that data under its own privacy policy.

iCloud is separate from the AI connection mirror. Enabling iCloud alone does not mean Repera runs a server that reads your history. If you also connect an AI assistant, server processing applies (section 9).

6. Apple Health / HealthKit (and future Android equivalents)

With your explicit permission, Repera may read and/or write health and activity data (e.g. weight, active energy, workouts, heart rate or routes) to show metrics and enrich progress in the App.

  • Health data is not used for advertising or commercial third-party tracking.
  • It is not sold or transferred to ad networks.
  • The AI assistant connection does not include Apple Health, sleep, HRV, GPS/routes or private notes under the current MCP contract.
  • You can revoke access in system Settings (Health / App permissions).

7. Camera and photos

If you use the whiteboard scanner, the App needs camera or photo-library access to read text from the image on device. Photos are processed for the import flow; they are not sent to a cloud AI service as part of the current scanner design.

8. Purchases and subscriptions (Repera Pro)

Subscriptions (Repera Pro) are handled via the App Store with StoreKit 2 and, when the Android version exists, Google Play Billing. The App verifies subscription status with the stores. That flow does not need your workout history or HealthKit data.

Repera does not use RevenueCat or another third-party subscription SDK on iOS. Apple and Google process payment data under their policies. Repera does not store card numbers.

Center-sponsored Pro access may exist via server checks distinct from store purchase; it does not replace AI-connection history processing.

9. AI assistant connection (optional)

You may authorise an external assistant (e.g. Claude or ChatGPT; others as available) to work with your journal via OAuth and consent at repera.app/ai, or via a personal token from Settings → Connect your AI. The AI connection requires a Repera account.

9.1 Infrastructure

  • Supabase (Repera project): authentication, AI-related data mirror, tokens/consents and Edge Functions. Documented project region: eu-west-1 (Ireland).
  • MCP server: Edge Function repera-mcp (read and write tools; verified in repo code: 39 tools — 22 read and 17 write). Consent distinguishes read vs write (“Allow creating and modifying my plan”).

9.2 Categories the assistant may use (aligned with MCP)

Based on published tools, the assistant may access, among other items:

  • Mirrored completed workout history (exercises, logged sets, WOD results), without HealthKit, GPS routes, energy, body weight or private notes.
  • Planning / calendar and AI plan proposals.
  • Per-exercise progress, muscle-balance estimates from mirrored history, and derived training analysis.
  • Exercise catalogue and equipment / discipline references.
  • Training preference profile you declare or confirm via the AI flow.
  • Custom exercises and pending change requests.
  • Programs / mesocycles created via the assistant, if you use that feature.

If you grant write permission, the assistant may create or modify plans and programs on the server, and request changes to completed workouts or custom exercises.

9.3 How changes reach your App

  • Plans / workouts proposed by the assistant: imported automatically when you open the App or sync (ai_training_plans inbox). There is currently no prior “approve plan” step before import.
  • Changes to completed workouts and custom exercises: remain pending and require your explicit approval in the App.

9.4 Recipients in this flow

  • Supabase: verified infrastructure for the mirror and MCP.
  • OpenAI / Anthropic (or another assistant provider): only if you connect that assistant; they process what the connector sends under their policies and your contract with them. Repera does not operate those models.

9.5 Revocation (reliable path)

To cut the assistant’s access to Repera, disable or delete the connection in the App: Settings → Connect your AI → My connections (Disable / delete). That revokes authorisation on the Repera side.

Removing only the connector inside ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant may not revoke authorisation in Repera and has caused “authorisation already used” errors. Always use the in-App path (or account deletion) for a reliable cut-off.

10. Analytics

In the App, product metrics go to Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics 4 (Repera Firebase project), without IDFA or advertising identifiers, and without Health, GPS, sets or free-text data. Details: project Analytics documentation.

On this website (repera.app) we use the same GA4 project via gtag only if you accept the “Analytics” cookie category. Default is denied (Consent Mode). We do not use that measurement for personalised advertising.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Local: while the App is installed or until you delete / uninstall.
  • iCloud: under Apple’s rules for your account.
  • Pro: cancelling the subscription does not delete workouts; it only re-applies free-plan limits.
  • Repera account / server: from Settings you can request account deletion. The server flow (repera-delete-account) deletes the Auth user and cascaded personal data (e.g. mirror, AI tokens, profile), with safeguards for already-authorised center histories (frozen read-only). Backup copies and infrastructure logs at the providers (Supabase, Google) follow their own retention and purge cycles after deletion.

12. Children

Repera is not directed at children under 16 (or the applicable digital age in your country). We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

13. Your rights (EU/EEA and Spain)

Where the GDPR or other applicable laws grant rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection, you may exercise them via the channel in section 1 and, where data lives only on your device or iCloud, manage it in the App or your Apple/Google account.

You may also revoke the AI connection (section 9.5) and request account deletion (section 11). You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

14. Strength reference datasets (third parties)

Some progress features (e.g. the Rendimiento block in Evolution) may show percentiles or guidance against aggregate strength references (squat, bench press, deadlift and, where applicable, other lifts), under an Amateur (gym) or Elite (competition) frame. Those aggregates are not your personal data: they are statistical tables shipped with the product (cache / Supabase) and, where authorised, may later be enriched with anonymous aggregated contributions from Repera users.

The competition reference baseline uses public results from OpenPowerlifting (public-domain / CC0 contributions). The gym reference baseline was incorporated via a one-time import of tables published by FitnessVolt Strength Standards (self-reported Symmetric Strength gym population), under CC BY 4.0. Repera does not continuously call the FitnessVolt API at runtime; the attribution required by that licence is maintained here and in the Terms of use.
